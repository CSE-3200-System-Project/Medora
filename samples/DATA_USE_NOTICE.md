# Prescription image data-use notice

The 105 prescription images in this directory are identifiable research material.
They are intended for public inclusion for transparent, reproducible optical
character recognition (OCR) research under written authorization that the authors
state permits permanent public download and inclusion in the Medora Zenodo
software archive. Before release, the archive record must identify the verified
approval authority, date, reference, and exact scope.

They are **not** covered by Medora's MIT software license or by the open license
applied to release-reviewed annotations and aggregate benchmark results. Raw OCR
or provider responses, prelabels, crops, bounding boxes, and other derived
artifacts that reproduce or reveal identifiers are governed by this same notice.
Access does not grant
permission to re-identify, contact, profile, discriminate against, or otherwise
harm a depicted or named person. Users must not combine the images with other data
for re-identification. Secondary use must remain within applicable law, ethics
requirements, and the scope of the underlying authorization.

The archive record must state the approval authority, approval date, reference,
and exact permitted scope. Those facts are deliberately not guessed in this file;
`tests/benchmarks/datasets/ocr_corpus_manifest.json` remains unfrozen until the
release manager enters and verifies them against the written approval.

Report suspected misuse or accidental exposure through the support contact in
`CITATION.cff`. Public availability cannot guarantee downstream deletion after a
download; this limitation must be explained to data subjects and archive users.
