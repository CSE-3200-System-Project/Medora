Prescription Rx review

You will see … prescription photographs. For each one, transcribe only the Rx section — the prescribed medicines and their instructions. Nothing else on the page.

What to transcribe

  • Include: everything written after the ℞ symbol — medicine names, strength, dose, frequency, duration, route, quantity, and instructions.
  • Exclude: patient name, age, sex, address, phone, ID numbers, dates, doctor name, degrees, registration number, chamber or hospital details, letterhead, signature, advice or follow-up notes outside the Rx list, and vitals.
  • If you cannot read something, type [illegible] in its place rather than guessing.

Images stay on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your work saves automatically after every record and you can close and reopen this page at any time.
